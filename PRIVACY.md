# Privacy Policy for Cygnus Player

**Effective Date:** July 25, 2026

Cygnus Player is designed with privacy as a primary principle. This app is a local-first audio player built to play media stored directly on your device.

## Data Collection and Transmission

- **No Personal Data Collected:** Cygnus Player does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or telemetry.
- **No External Servers:** The app does not communicate with external servers or third-party analytics services.
- **No Ads or Tracking:** Cygnus Player contains no advertisements, tracking SDKs, or third-party data collection frameworks.

## Device Permissions

To function as a media player, Cygnus Player requests access to your device's local storage and media library:

- **Storage / Media Access (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** This permission is strictly used on-device to index, locate, and play your audio files, as well as read metadata (such as track titles, album names, and embedded cover art). This data never leaves your device.
